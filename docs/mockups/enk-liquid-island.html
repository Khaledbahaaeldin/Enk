The writer island as liquid glass: which shape?

All three are the same liquid recipe (almost no blur, lens bending at the rim, light edge, colour fringe, shapes that melt into each other). They differ in how the island opens. Watch the loop, or hold the buttons below. The paper text runs under the island on purpose so you can see it bend.

This is a browser approximation (a WebGL lens with the same ingredients: rim bending, colour fringe, light on the rim, no blur), not the phone renderer: the real one is Backdrop's lens, which is sharper and follows your finger. Judge the shape and the motion here; the material itself we tune on the device.
