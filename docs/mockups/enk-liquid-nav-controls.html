The nav bar in liquid glass: which one?

Drag along each bar. At rest the selected tab sits under a soft pill; as soon as you touch it, it turns into a clear lens that swells past the bar, bends the icons under it and leans after your finger, then drops onto the nearest tab. Near another glass shape it melts into it.

Controls: thumbs, toggle and buttons

The Catalog's behaviour: a slider thumb or toggle bead is a white capsule at rest; grab it and it turns into a clear, bigger lens you can see the track through, stretching with your speed. The round + glows where you press. Drag and click them.

A browser approximation of the same recipe (WebGL lens: rim bending, colour fringe, lit rim, almost no blur). The real renderer is Backdrop on the phone; judge layout and behaviour here.
