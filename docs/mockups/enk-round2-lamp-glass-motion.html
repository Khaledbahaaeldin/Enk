Enk round 2 mockups

Two interactive mockups for the decisions in the round 2 spec: the lamp with its glass pop-up, and the motion set for the shell. A browser cannot bend light through glass the way the app will, so the glass here shows blur, tint, highlights and movement, not true refraction.

  • Tap the bulb in the top bar to open the pop-up. Tap outside to close it.
  • Drag a slider. The thumb grows on touch, stretches with speed, and the page changes under your finger.
  • Drag the marker along the rail. The pool of light moves with it.
  • Switch the lamp on and off to see the filament warm up and cool down.

A and B use the same light: one source on the carriage, brightness falling with distance, a cone set by Beam, colour set by temperature. They differ only in what the ink does. "Today" is the current build: a blurred trapezoid, a circle, and an oval mask on the text.

  • Press anything. It reacts when the finger lands, not when it lifts.
  • Tap or drag the nav bar. The lens follows the finger, stretches with speed, and a flick carries it.
  • Tap a note. The card grows into the paper. "Box" sends it back.
  • Hold a note (or right-click). The menu grows from your finger. "Move to Trash" shows the alert, and the card folds away.
  • Setup > New divider. The sheet rises; drag or flick it down.
  • Switch to Today and do the same things. That is the current build.

Every movement is a spring that can be grabbed or redirected mid-way. Tap two tabs quickly, or grab the sheet while it is still rising.
