Enk: three ways into the writer

Tap Write in the glass bar of each phone to play the transition; tap the island (or "Back to Box") to run it in reverse. Every transition is driven by one spring, so you can interrupt it mid-flight: tap again while it moves and it reverses from where it is. Drag the scrubber to inspect any frame. The glass bends what is behind it (Chromium browsers).
