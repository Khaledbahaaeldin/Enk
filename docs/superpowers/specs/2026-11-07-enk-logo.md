# Enk logo

Date: 2026-11-07. Status: approved by the author (variant B, "Rail and caret", with the bold sans wordmark). Reference sheets: `docs/mockups/enk-logo-final.html` (construction) and `docs/mockups/enk-logo.html` (the three explored variants).

## Idea

The symbol is the app's signature moment frozen: a typed line, the orange caret that follows it, and the carriage riding the steel rail directly beneath. The wordmark repeats the caret as its full stop. Nothing in the logo is invented for the logo; every part exists in the writing screen (design system sections 6.2 to 6.5).

## Rules

1. The orange (`#FF5A1F`) appears only in the caret and the carriage's indicator strip. The wordmark's caret is the same orange in every theme (it is a brand mark, not a theme accent).
2. The symbol sits inside the 72 dp visible circle of the 108 dp adaptive grid; only the rail runs past it and is clipped by the mask.
3. No text inside the launcher icon. The wordmark is used inside the app, on the splash, About, store art, and the README.
4. Never recolor the steel or the graphite. Never add a second accent.
5. Not a copy of any Platen asset; it uses Enk's own rail, carriage, and caret.

| Part | Value |

| Typed lines | Three bars, height 2.6, radius 1.3, fill `#F3EEE3` at 34% opacity: x36 y24 w28; x36 y29.5 w22; x36 y35 w18 |

| Rail | x6 to x102, y56, h8.5; vertical gradient `#F4F4F2` 0%, `#A9A9A6` 40%, `#6F6F6C` 62%, `#C9C9C6` 100%; soft shadow below (y57.5, h4, black 42%, blur 1.7) |
| Carriage | The writer carriage (design system 6.3) at scale 1.25, centre (61, 60.25): body 30 x 21 with collars 3, grooves, slot 10.4 x 7 with slug, orange strip 8.6 x 1.9; soft shadow below (ellipse, black 55%, blur 1.7) |

## Wordmark

"enk" in **Inter Bold** (SIL OFL 1.1, bundled and credited), lowercase, outlined to paths in the SVG, tracking -4.5%. The caret is a rounded bar 0.34 em wide and 0.085 em tall, 0.06 em to the right of the k, sitting just below the baseline like a typed underscore. Colors: `#F3EEE3` on dark, `#2B2824` on light, caret `#FF5A1F` on both. Minimum wordmark width 56 dp. Clear space around any lockup equals the carriage body width (about 37 units of the symbol) or the wordmark's x-height.
