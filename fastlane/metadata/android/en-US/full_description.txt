Enk is an offline, private typewriter notebook for Android designed for focused, deliberate writing.

What it is:
• A mechanical typewriter writing experience with tactile acoustic feedback, moving carriage rail, margin return bell, and night lamp.
• Two ways to correct yourself: clean erase (the default) or strike-through, which keeps your corrections on the page like a real typed draft.
• A physical note organization system: file notes into Dividers in the Box, search by text match, and review trashed pages.
• Fully offline: zero internet permission declared in the manifest, zero background tracking, and zero analytics.
• Clean export and import: backup your notes as standard ZIP archives at any time.

What it is not:
• It is not a cloud-synchronized notebook. Notes live entirely on your device.
• It is not a rich-text or markdown word processor. Every character is monospaced type on virtual paper.
